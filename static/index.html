📜 Studio Guidelines

⏱ Punctuality & Grace Period
To respect each client's dedicated private session, a 15-minute grace period is provided. A $10 late fee applies after 15 minutes. Being 30+ minutes late requires rescheduling.
🚿 Clean Hair Standard
Hair must be clean and freshly shampooed prior to your service to protect blade precision, or you may add our signature Royal Scalp Shampoo treatment.
🌙 After-Hours VIP Sessions
A $50 after-hours fee applies for requested slots outside standard hours, ensuring private 1-on-1 studio lockdown.
💳 Payment Acceptance
Balances and purchases can be completed with Cash, Zelle, Cash App, or Credit/Debit card at checkout.
